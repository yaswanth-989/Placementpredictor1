Probability Calibration & Reliability Analysis

📈 Calibration
A well-calibrated classifier outputs true posterior probabilities: if a student is assigned a 0.80 placement probability, exactly 80% of students in that bin should indeed get placed. Tree ensembles often exhibit uncalibrated sigmoidal probability outputs that benefit from Platt scaling or Isotonic regression.
