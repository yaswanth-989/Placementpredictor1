🌀 t-SNE
t-SNE converts high-dimensional student Euclidean distances into conditional probabilities and minimizes Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence in 2D space. It is exceptionally skilled at revealing isolated sub-populations and complex non-linear groupings.
